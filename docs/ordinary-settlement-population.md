# Ordinary settlement population

2026-09-28 policy · Verified 2026-09-29 · Implements the user's average-seven, low-wealth-four, wealthy-up-to-twelve policy. Applies to newly generated eligible ordinary faction settlements only. Quest-location integration is planned separately under P4D.

## Budget policy

Vanilla's defender combat-point budget is not used by this generator. Its relative wealth means **declared spend capacity**, not the player's wealth, physical silver, canonical trade-stock value or an off-map economic simulation. P5 still owns canonical stock/trade consistency.

The existing profile supplies the base equipment/import allowances. A standard resident-kit allowance is the larger of the profile's per-resident equipment allowance and the stage/climate per-resident default. Normal wealth funds seven such allowances; a saved, independently seeded wealth multiplier adjusts the equipment budget. Population is the number of standard allowances it can fund, capped at twelve. Less than four funded residents is invalid, not a reason to mint budget. The import allowance uses the same wealth multiplier but stays separate from resident equipment; normal wealth retains the existing profile import limit.

The provisional distribution is calibrated to an exact expected population of seven, not a promise that every small set of world tiles averages seven:

| Relative wealth | Weight | Funded residents |
|---|---|---|
| 60% | 11% | 4 |
| 75% | 14% | 5 |
| 90% | 18% | 6 |
| 100% | 22% | 7 |
| 120% | 14% | 8 |
| 135% | 8% | 9 |
| 150% | 6% | 10 |
| 165% | 4% | 11 |
| 180% | 3% | 12 |

Tech and climate change the cost of a standard kit, not these population targets. This avoids making all high-tech settlements large or all tribal settlements small. Resident equipment is still selected/degraded/validated against the actual allowance. Local materials and physical power/import limits continue to constrain construction and optional defenses; this policy does not invent a new construction-value ledger or claim every site can support twelve residents.

## Generation and compatibility

`OrdinarySettlementPopulation` is pure. `P4AMapPlanner` gets the stable saved wealth choice from `P0WorldState`, computes its allowances, then passes it to `LayoutRequirements.FromProfile` before site/room/field/defense planning. Beds, crop nutrition, dining/storage/medical capacities, excavation allowance, loadouts, medicine/component stocks and population-scaled startup food follow the actual resident count. Mortar/support roles come from that roster; no additional unfunded pawns are spawned. Existing fixed combat-reserve formulas are unchanged and are not a claim of longer sustainable operation.

`ordinaryWealthPercent` is serialized per settlement. Newly generated identities select once from a purpose-separated seed; reload/regeneration reuse the saved value rather than rerolling with current player wealth. States already generated by the old mod are marked legacy and keep their four-resident profile. Existing loaded pawns/maps are not modified. Casualty identity and full revisit reconciliation remain the separately recorded P5 limitation.

The authoritative `PROCEDURAL_PLAN` log includes residents, population policy, wealth percentage, equipment allowance and import allowance. Failed geometry must remain visible; never silently shrink the funded roster to hide a layout rejection.

## Reserve space before packing

The initial compound envelope uses the minimum bounds area of **all** required facilities, including power/fuel storage, with the existing circulation allowance. It now adds two cells to both reservation axes per resident above four. This is a provisional packing allowance, not an obligation to fill the entire envelope with buildings. A bigger envelope alone did not solve the reproduced rejection: the field-facing gate could remain offset or blocked after rooms were packed.

New population-policy plans therefore establish a connected field-approach branch and keep its outward crossing free **before** placing rooms. Multi-layer targets get a wider gate frontage; the chosen gate is checked for connected wall returns. A wall arm can shorten to a legal return rather than duplicate an irregular baseline wall. Protected service lanes stay reserved against construction, but are not treated as physical obstacles where they share the gate-to-field crossing. Actual structures and explicit obstacles on those cells still reject the attachment.

If coarse field sampling finds no feasible field/compound pair, one cell-resolution field-only refinement is allowed. Both phases retain their pair-evaluation bounds, and origin scanning is bounded by map area. Same-site growth retains the chosen field/interfaces; larger-population reservations can extend on other free axes when one proposed growth axis reaches a map edge. Neither refinement nor growth relaxes terrain, crop capacity, path or material validation.

New ordinary plans prefer stockpile rooms of up to 48 cells, splitting larger capacities without discarding stock space. The bounded split count still permits larger legal rooms when necessary; legacy requirements retain their old split policy. Default packing-work ceilings scale with supported population (400,000 evaluations at four, 1,225,000 at seven, 3,600,000 at twelve; eight/fourteen/twenty-four repairs per attempt), while explicit caller limits take precedence. The clutter regression below deliberately retains the **original 400,000-evaluation/eight-repair limit** to demonstrate that the upfront geometry fix does not depend on a longer search.

Runtime stocks pool **all** matching storage/food/medical rooms instead of filling only the first split room. `LayoutStockCells` prefers planned shelves, then designated stockpile cells; circulation and interaction cells are not overflow storage. Shelf placement honors the actual storage filters and live per-cell stack limit. Shelves are `PassThroughOnly`, so their cells must be walkable rather than strictly standable. Finite item counts, stuff and quality remain unchanged; the pure capacity calculation conservatively counts one stack per shelf cell, not the extra live shelf slots.

Optional exterior-wall layers may fall back through the existing production policy; the funded population, room capacities, crop demand and equipment/import allowances may not. These changes do not establish universal acceptance on every flat tile or complete P4/P4A/P4B's remaining live/optional-mod matrix.

## Verification

Synthetic tests cover every wealth roll across both profiles, all stages and temperate/cold/hot climates; affordability boundaries and the exact expected average; stable saved choices and legacy migration; initial reservation growth; and complete attached-field layouts/loadouts for four, seven and twelve residents.

The fixed 160×160 boulder/water/plant fixture accepts all three funded rosters within the original search bound. Nine population/target-layer combinations preserve their entire roster and use explicit optional-layer fallback where needed. On this fixture the effective layers for targets 1/2/3 are respectively 1/2/2 at four residents, 1/2/3 at seven, and 1/1/1 at twelve. The twelve-resident single-layer pack uses 18,338 evaluations; simply enlarging the envelope before protecting the connection failed after about 3.4 million evaluations. A separate clear 180×180 rich fixture accepts twelve residents with both two and three physical wall layers, legal returns and attached field paths. Replaying its field connector with protected service lanes succeeds; adding actual obstacles on those crossings rejects it.

On 2026-09-29 the final complete LayoutTests suite passed with zero failures (132,637 ms), the complete PlannerTests suite passed with zero failures, and the Docker Release mod build succeeded with zero warnings/errors. The older high-industrial flat-density matrix retained 12/12 acceptance; default/rich turret fixtures retained four/six guns. A new storage regression covers four/seven/eight/twelve residents, legal shelf-first selection, every split room, no duplicated/foreign/access cells, and sufficient conservative capacity for finite loadout/fuel stocks.
